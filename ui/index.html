    Activation

    Manual: Aura listens only after you press the orb or hold the hotkey.

    Phrase gate

    In always-listening mode, the transcript must begin with this phrase — a second factor that kills false wakes.

    Train your own phrase

    1. Pick a phrase that's yours — “hey aura”, “okay mac”, anything distinct.
    2. Run python scripts/train_wakeword.py "your phrase".
    3. Drop the ONNX into your data folder and add its path to [wake] models in config.toml.

    openWakeWord training runs locally, ~20 minutes on any Mac.
